Sheherazade's Lantern

Ancient tales from the Arabian nights, rekindled for modern souls — anime-style audio drama with full voice cast.

10 Episodes
Episode 1
The Map of the Moving Streets
Rashid the Mapmaker draws cities that change overnight. Yueya the astronomer discovers his maps match a sky that hasn't risen yet. Together they find the Brass City — built by King Solomon's djinn.
🎭 Rashid (Irish) · Yueya (British)
Episode 2
The Spice of Forgotten Things
A city is forgetting itself. Karim meets Nadia the Spice Merchant, who offers him a taste of every life he never lived.
🎭 Nadia (American) · Karim (British)
Episode 3
The Blade That Was Never Finished
A haunted swordsmith. A boy seeking revenge. An unfinished blade on the wall. Khalid keeps a secret that the forge fire cannot burn away.
🎭 Khalid (American deep voice)
Episode 4
The Book of Things Never Said
Once a century, a door appears in the Caravanserai. Inside waits Dunyazad, Scheherazade's sister, with a book of blank pages that holds every unspoken truth.
🎭 Dunyazad (Indian ethereal) · Yueya (British)
Episode 5
The Sand That Sang Twice
Deep in the Empty Quarter, Karim hears the dunes singing. Layla holds a single grain of sand that reveals what he has been searching for his entire life.
🎭 Layla (Australian) · Karim (British)
Episode 6
The Spice That Remembered
Nadia finds a black peppercorn that opens a door to the Black Bazaar — a market that exists between worlds. She and Rashid enter together, and discover that the most precious thing in the shadow market isn't for sale: it's the memory of something you lost before you were born.
🎭 Nadia (American) · Rashid (Irish)
Episode 7
The Song That Unmade a Sword
Layla of the Singing Dunes arrives at Khalid's forge with a single grain of sand that carries a melody from before the world was made. The song has the power to finish what he started forty years ago — but only if he is willing to let his unfinished blade be broken first.
🎭 Khalid (American deep voice) · Layla (Australian)
Episode 8
The Story That Read Her Back
Yueya has forgotten something she can't name — a star, a story, a voice that taught her the constellations. Dunyazad returns with a silver page that reads the reader instead of being read. To find what she lost, Yueya must let the story see her first.
🎭 Yueya (British) · Dunyazad (Indian ethereal)
Episode 9
The Debt That Travels on the Wind
Karim receives an object from the northern pass — a debt so old the ink has faded from his memory. Nadia's pepper box holds the scent of a promise made in a snowstorm, on a mountain where a young woman kept a shelter warm for strangers. The high pass remembers. The debt is due.
🎭 Karim (British) · Nadia (American)
Episode 10
The Elder and the Oracle
Karim's compass has been spinning for three days — pointing nowhere at all. Layla left him a desert rose years ago, with a message: 'Give this to the traveller whose compass stops spinning.' He returns to the Singing Dunes one last time, to ask the question he has been avoiding for thirty-seven years: when is it time to stop?
🎭 Karim (British) · Layla (Australian)
